I’m A-Thrice

Listen / buy this song on Bandcamp ↗

About this song

An opening statement about identity made through repeated migration. Schoolroom misrecognition becomes an account of family routes, paperwork and self-authorship, with the name A-Thrice as a refusal to fit a single category.
