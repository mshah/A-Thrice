A Tribe Called 44

Listen / buy this song on Bandcamp ↗

About this song

A comeback to the question “What tribe?” that answers with history rather than defensiveness. The number forty-four becomes both a punchline and a historical correction, linking a personal slight to migration, inherited routes and formal recognition.
