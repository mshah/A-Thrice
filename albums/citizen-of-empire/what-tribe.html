What Tribe?

Listen / buy this song on Bandcamp ↗

About this song

A confrontation with the question itself. The song exposes how casual questions can become demands for proof of belonging, then turns the scrutiny back on the person asking. The satire moves from interview-room absurdity toward a wider challenge to the language of exclusion.
