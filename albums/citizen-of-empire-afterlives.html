01 / INSIDE THE RECORD

Why this album exists.

Afterlives expands the first Citizen of Empire project into a panoramic investigation of imperial inheritance. The record moves between the personal, the domestic and the infrastructural: passports and trade routes, museum display cases, the punishment economy, household language, diaspora memory, code and the many cultures now sharing London. It ultimately asks what might replace empire—and suggests that shared culture is already writing different answers.

The original album began with a person being misread. This record asks what built the categories in the first place, how those systems persist and what people can build beyond them.
