01 / INSIDE THE RECORD

Why this album exists.

Panic by Design examines fear as an ecosystem rather than an isolated emotion. News cycles, identity verification, school safety rituals, digital permissions, surveillance and corporate disclaimers become parts of a system that rewards vigilance and makes compliance feel ordinary. The record moves from an overstimulating newsfeed toward the quieter costs of living with an always-on alarm.

I wanted to ask not simply why we are anxious, but who benefits when fear becomes an industry, an interface and a normal expectation of modern citizenship.

NARRATIVE ARCMedia overload → procedural control → intimate human cost → lucidity and refusal.
