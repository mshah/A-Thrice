HOME IS MORE THAN A RETURN ADDRESS

01 / THE PROJECT

What do we carry
when we move?

Return to Sender is a contemporary sculptural, textile, sonic and archival installation conceived by A-Thrice. Following journeys through the Indian Ocean, East Africa and Britain, it asks what migration takes, what it makes possible and how ordinary objects become vessels for complicated histories.

The project brings together postal routes, trunks, patterned fabric, maritime landscapes and family memory. Its materials invite a closer look at the institutions and infrastructures through which people and goods have travelled.
