# Out of Frame — Exhibition Website v1.5

## The Hunt and the Photograph

Built from website v1.4.2. Adds a historically grounded reading section to each artwork page between the panorama and the numbered research chapters. Each includes an original-photograph view, historical occasion, people and organisation, exposure/attribution limits and institutional reading links.

### Buffalo Corner (Study 01)
Roosevelt's 1909–10 expedition, May 1909 Kamiti visit, the five men and their different roles, transport and specimen work, and the original photograph's uncertain camera operator. Based on Buffalo Corner Master Annotation Packet v4.0, the Library of Congress record, Smithsonian expedition record and African Game Trails.

### The Royal Ring (Study 02)
Delhi Durbar/December 1911 Nepal visit, Chandra Shumsher as diplomatic host, the elephant-ring system, original photograph vs. absent quarry, album circulation and unknown exposure photographer. Based on Royal Ring Master Annotation Packet v0.4 and ANU/Asian Art Museum collection records.

### Other changes

### Editorial safeguards
- Buffalo Corner: institutional attribution to Kermit is not proof he operated the camera in which he is depicted; LOC 1919 date is a later copyright registration.
- Royal Ring: the specific exposure photographer, original mark '156', date and number of elephants beyond the original frame are unresolved. Brooks's camera role in the reconstructed panorama is expressly artistic.
- Neither artwork implies a recovered wide-angle negative, a simultaneous montage, or individually authenticated figures in the reconstructed surround.
- No changes made to the four v0.1 curatorial essay drafts.
